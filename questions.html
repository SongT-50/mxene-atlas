Q1

A free-standing film of Ti₃C₂Tₓ whose surface groups were partly replaced by covalently attached alkoxide / aryloxide groups (ethoxide / phenoxide). Would you count its conductivity as a pure MXene film value?

Example from a real paper (no need to open it)
Alkoxide groups, such as ethoxide and phenoxide, are covalently attached to Ti 3 C 2 T x MXene surfaces using a nucleophilic substitution mechanism, enabling stable dispersion in both polar and non‐polar solvents.
Alkoxides, being strong nucleophiles, are capable of forming covalent bonds to the titanium atoms on the Ti 3 C 2 T x MXene surface by replacing the ‐OH surface group.

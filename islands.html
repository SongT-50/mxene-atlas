MOF·COF islands NEW

Two new islands next to the MXene map — metal–organic frameworks (MOF) and covalent organic frameworks (COF), BET surface area · MOF CO₂ uptake (new Oct 23).

Status — values were extracted from papers by AI and kept only when two different AI judges agreed the quoted sentence supports them. No domain expert has checked them yet. We re-read 120 randomly chosen passed rows against the papers and found 8 wrong (removed). Please read these as «passed AI judging», not «verified».
This is a first version. When experts reply, or we find better evidence, we will change it — and record the date and the reason on this page.

Defaults we chose (may change)

  • A value reported only as «surface area» with no model name is not counted as BET.
  • Materials derived from MOFs (e.g. MOF-derived carbons) are excluded (193 rows).
  • Pore volume is not shown yet — our check found definition errors (micropore volume in the total-pore-volume column); we will add it after fixing the definition.
  • The framework that links metal units by imine bonds (JNM-3) is marked «MOF/COF hybrid».
  • CTFs (triazine frameworks) are counted as COFs when the authors call them CTF or COF. Because some argue that high-temperature CTFs are closer to amorphous carbon, they are shown as a separate «CTF» group (Oct 23).
  • The judges tend to accept «surface area» as BET in papers that use BET. In the second COF round, 3 of 20 sampled rows had only «surface area» without «BET» in the sentence (removed). Some may remain outside the sample.
  • Sample types: pure · composite (mixed with another material) · modified (guest, doping, degradation) · after use (measured after adsorption/regeneration) · needs check. Default view = pure.

Same name, very different values — half explained

For the same MOF name, BET values differed tens to hundreds of times across papers (pure samples only). On Oct 23 we fixed the name groups, and a large part turned out to be «different materials sharing a name»: MIL-53 ×244 → Al form ×4.7 · Fe form ×7.5 · MOF-74 ×240 → per metal (Ni ×4.6 · Mg ×1.9 …) · ZIF-8 ×152 → ×43 (the ZIF-L phase taken out). The large spreads that remain look like sample and condition differences — UiO-67 (measured after soaking in buffer) · MOF-74(Fe) · MIL-125 (6, 12 and 18 h synthesis). CO₂ uptake (MOF) NEW Oct 23

This is the second property of the MOF island. It shows equilibrium uptake from a single-gas (CO₂) isotherm, per mass of sample only. The first judging round (M5) failed. The two AI judges read one line of the judging instructions differently. We added a clarification to that line and re-ran only the judging (M5b). This time it passed (supported 89.5% · wrong 8.6%). We re-read 20 randomly chosen passed rows against the papers and found 0 wrong. For the «CO₂ uptake» rows alone, however, 12.5% were judged wrong. That is above the 10% round threshold. The table below lists only rows that both judges supported. No domain expert has checked them yet.
  • The mmol/g column is our conversion. We divided mg/g by 44.01 and cm³/g by 22.414 (reading cm³ as STP volume). We did not convert wt%, because papers use different denominators, so that cell is left empty. The value as written is in the next column.
  • Values measured at different temperatures or pressures cannot be compared. Pick a condition group first.
  • Isosteric heat, selectivity and breakthrough capacity passed judging but are not shown yet.
  • Materials made by burning MOFs are excluded (40 rows).
